\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

This paper presents a modular \LaTeX{} structure configured under the 7th edition of the APA guidelines using the \texttt{apa7} class. The manuscript has been divided into thematic modules and includes the configuration for a workflow assisted by artificial intelligence agents specialized in writing, citation, review, and style auditing.

\indent\textit{Keywords:} APA 7 Guidelines, LaTeX, Modular Architecture, Scientific Writing, AI Agents
