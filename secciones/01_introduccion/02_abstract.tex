\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

This project develops a web and mobile information system to optimize the application and selection process for the \enquote{Beca Comedor} (Dining Hall Scholarship) at the Universidad Autónoma Gabriel René Moreno (UAGRM), managed by the University Directorate of Social Welfare and Health (DUBSS). Currently, the review of about 20,000 application folders per academic term is done manually, which causes bottlenecks, long response times, and applications that are never reviewed. Students also lack a unified digital channel to apply and to check the status of their application.

The solution consists of a web portal for administrative management and a mobile application for students. Its core is an Artificial Intelligence model that pre-selects applications, computes a socioeconomic vulnerability score, and prioritizes the folders that staff must audit. Additionally, a Business Intelligence module provides interactive dashboards with indicators on pre-selection and dining hall usage, supporting decision-making by the authorities.

Development follows the Unified Software Development Process (PUDS), modeled with UML, and requirements are specified according to the IEEE 830 standard. The expected result is a faster, more transparent, and auditable scholarship process.

\indent\textit{Keywords: Information System, Artificial Intelligence, Business Intelligence, PUDS, IEEE 830.}
